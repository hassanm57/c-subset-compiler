.\compiler.exe -tac -no-opt demo.c   ← show raw TAC (THREE ADDRESS CODE) (skips opt)
.\compiler.exe -opt demo.c           ← show optimized TAC (cleaner, fewer steps)
.\compiler.exe -ast demo.c           ← show the full syntax tree
.\compiler.exe -sym demo.c           ← show symbol table with all names
